Step 3 · Connect it to the productThe product

Meet Ava, TAP’s AI Interviewer

Ava interviews every applicant by voice, on their own phone, whenever they want, using questions built from RGF’s job descriptions. For technicians she shows a real scenario and asks what’s wrong. Recruiters get scored, ranked results, and a person makes every decision.

Screens for skill

An adaptive technical conversation built from your job profiles and scoring rubrics, instead of résumé keywords.

Shows real equipment

“You should be seeing a conveyor belt. Do you notice any issues?” Candidates reason through the fault out loud.

Any phone, any time

No scheduling and no phone tag. Candidates interview around their current shift.

Fair and consistent

The same job-based questions and rubric for everyone. A person on Dana’s team decides.

Questions

Straight answers to the questions we usually get

“We’ve been pitched a lot of HR tools. Why is this different?”

Don’t take our word for it. Take the first question yourself, then judge it on RGF’s 12-week numbers. Most HR tools manage the process. Ava tests whether the person can do the job.

“Will candidates hate talking to an AI?”

They interview on their own phone, on their own schedule, with no phone tag. The pilot measures completion rates and candidate feedback, so you’ll see the answer in your own data.

“Will my recruiters actually use it?”

It takes away their least-liked task, the first-round phone screen. Recruiters help calibrate the rubric, and they start each day with a ranked shortlist.

“Does Ava work in Spanish?”

Korey will confirm current language support with TAP’s product team and come back with a clear answer, including whether it’s on the roadmap.

“How do you stop someone from cheating?”

Scenario questions are hard to fake: candidates have to reason through a fault they can see. TAP’s current identity-check approach will be covered in scoping.

“What does it cost?”

Pilot pricing is scoped to the roles and volume. We price against the cost of a bad technician hire, not per interview.

“Does it connect to our ATS?”

ATS and HRIS integration is part of pilot setup. Tell us which system RGF uses, and we’ll confirm the connection during scoping.

“Can Ava do something we’d love to see?”

Tell us. Every feature request goes to TAP’s product team with RGF’s use case, and you’ll get an honest answer on if and when.
